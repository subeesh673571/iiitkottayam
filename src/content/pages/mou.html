Industry Academic Collaboration

between

Technolodge, Kakkor P.O, Piravom, Ernakulam

and

The Indian Institute of information Technology Kottayam

IIIT Kottayam signed an MoU with Technolodge, Kakkoor, Ernakulam on 25th November 2019 for promoting the industry-academy interaction activities and to develop the academic and entrepreneurial excellence with the following broad objectives:

  • To effectively share the facilities and expertise for improving the capabilities for advanced education, research and consultancy work.
  • Collaborate to share and exchange professsional and useful information between both parties for mutual benefit and knowledge enhancement.
  • To enable the use of laboratories and test facilities on a preferential and professional basis and concessional rate by Technolodge and vice versa.
  • To provide opportunity for students of IIITK to undertake industrial training and projects in Technolodge for mutual benefit.
  • To encourage the training for the employees of technolodge in Continuing Education and skill enhancement activities on preferential basis.
