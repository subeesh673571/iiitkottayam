Student Co-operative Mess

Introduction
The Student Co-operative Mess is a student-run initiative committed to providing nutritious, hygienic, and affordable meals to the campus community. It operates on a non-profit basis, aiming to create a welcoming dining environment where students can enjoy a variety of cuisines catering to different tastes and dietary needs.
Key Features
  • Affordable Dining: The mess operates with a focus on keeping meal costs low while ensuring high-quality and diverse food options.
  • Student Management: Managed by a committee of elected student representatives, including the Mess Secretary, who oversee daily operations, menu planning, budgeting, and quality control. The faculty in charge provides proper guidance and support to ensure smooth operations.
  • Nutritious Meals: Offers balanced meals with both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options. The menu is regularly rotated to ensure variety, with special dishes available during festivals and events.
  • Hygiene and Quality Assurance: Adheres to strict hygiene standards, with regular quality checks to ensure meals meet health and safety guidelines.
