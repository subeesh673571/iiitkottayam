Note 1: All students must have a laptop at the time of admission with a minimum configuration of: i5/i7 processor or equivalent with at least 8 GB RAM, and Linux Installation (UBUNTU 22.04 or higher preferred).

Note 2: While you report at the institute, hostel rooms will be equipped with a mattress, pillow, bucket, and mug. All are to purchase these items mandatorily from the security desk of respective hostels for a cost of Rs 2,650. This is to maintain parity among students and also to avoid parents and students wandering for purchases in nearby cities and to reduce conveyance and transportation issues.

Note 3:All students are required to submit valid vaccination proof for Chickenpox and Hepatitis A at the time of reporting to the Institute. Students who have not yet received these vaccinations must get vaccinated before reporting to the Institute. (Students who are unable to produce vaccination certificates must submit IgG antibody test reports for Chickenpox (Varicella IgG) and Hepatitis A (HAV IgG) issued by NABL-accredited laboratories.)
