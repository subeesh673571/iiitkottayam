Winner of ACM-ICPC Asia Chennai Regional Contest 2019

Hosted at Hindustan Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai

IIT Kharagpur, IIT Varanasi, IIIT Allahabad, NIT Surathkal and other renowned institutes of India participated in ACM-ICPC Asia Chennai Regional Contest 2019 along with IIIT-Kottayam. Our 6 teams participated and 4 teams bags the top 25 positions in India.

It’s a matter of great surprise that if we compare the institute wise performance then we are topping the list.

  • One team of IIT Varanasi (Topper)
  • One team of IIT Kharagpur (Second Topper)
  • Two Teams of NIT Surathkal
  • Three Teams of IIIT-Allahabad…..
  • Four Teams of IIIT-Kottayam
