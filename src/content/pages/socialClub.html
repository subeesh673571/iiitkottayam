    • This is an official social club of IIIT Kottayam, that focusses on societal impact of IIIT Kottayam by organizing various programs round the year. Some notable events include: Swachhata Hi Seva, Chitrachaya induction, Trivia Quiz, Its debatable, blood donation camp etc.

    This club is a union various sub-clubs like Chitrachaya (for photography), Literary club (for quizzes and appreciating literary works), Manga club etc. The Trendles Club of IIIT Kottayam is a vibrant and dynamic group dedicated to fostering community engagement and social responsibility among students. The club organizes a wide array of activities, including blood donation camps, cleanliness drives, and literary programs such as quizzes, puzzles, and photographic contests. These initiatives aim to promote a sense of civic duty, intellectual curiosity, and creative expression, creating a well-rounded and socially aware student body. Through its diverse events, the Societal Club strives to make a positive impact both within the campus and the wider community, embodying the spirit of service and excellence.

    Be it Adaviya a freshers’ night to welcome freshers, or reminiscence of India-Pakistan Partition Day disaster, or Environmental Day celebrations or flash-mobbing or conducting quizzes or cleanliness drive, Trendles club is the one that has the deep-root in.

    Join hands with us; get refreshed, feel content and feel responsible with the wide-spread social activities of the Trendles club.
