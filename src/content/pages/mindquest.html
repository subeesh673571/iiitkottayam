    About Us

    Mind Quest is a club that promotes mental well being, and is a place to let go of all your stress and worries. Our mission is not just promote mental health in the college but in various disciplines with the help of out reach programs. Not just do we have promote mental well being but we also host activities for students to relieve themselves from pressures of studies like game nights, fun talks and other creative solutions that we are constantly coming up with.

    Our Vision

    To create a space for all students and foster a stress free environment

    Our Goals
    • Focusing on the mental wellness of students.
    • Stigma reduction and breaking barriers to promote help seeking behavior.
    • Emotional well being practices.
    • Focuses on reducing stress and anxiety issues among students.
    • Addressing any kind of harassment and viola5on.
    • Awareness for using drugs and other substances .
    • Addressing and reducing Suicidal idea5on.
    • Develop self awareness.
    • Create self efficacy :- be informed - educate yourself on mental health issues.
    • Be proactive – implement protective factors such as yoga, deep breathing, regular physical activity, effective communication.
    • Learn Productivity hacks – Speed Reading, Time management, Super memory, creative thinking.
    • Overcome your fears – stage fear, public speaking, conversation with a stranger or a person you always dream to talk to.
    • Provide support and mentorship for anyone suffering from mental health issues and bring the best out of them .
    • Make a community of happy minds.
