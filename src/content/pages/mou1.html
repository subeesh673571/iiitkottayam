MoU with Atal Incubation Centre- IIITK Foundation & Aster Digital Health Incubator

Atal Incubation Centre- IIITK Foundation and Aster Digital Health Incubator agreed to build cooperation by signing a memorandum of understanding (MoU) on "center of excellence support and promote innovation and entrepreneurship in digital health". This MoU will further boost mutual collaboration in the area of digital health and innovation between the two institutions.
