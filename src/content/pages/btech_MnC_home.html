B.Tech. in Mathematics and Computing

Welcome to a program that bridges the gap between abstract mathematical theory and real-world computational power. Our B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing (MnC) is designed for students who want to go beyond simply using technology; they want to build the algorithms that define it. By combining the analytical depth of mathematics with the practical versatility of computer science, we prepare graduates to tackle the world's most complex challenges in AI, fintech, and data science. Students will not just learn to code; they will gain an understanding of the mathematical foundations that make programming possible. In a rapidly changing job market, versatility is the greatest asset. The B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing offers a unique advantage by providing a solid foundation in mathematical logic that never becomes obsolete. This program opens doors to high-impact careers in quantitative finance, pharmaceutical research, and software engineering. This is more than just a degree; it is a pathway to a secure and prestigious global career. We recognize that today’s industry demands more than just developers—it needs problem solvers who understand system integrity. At the core of this program is a commitment to excellence and interdisciplinary collaboration, empowering the next generation of thinkers to solve complex global challenges through algorithmic rigor and system resilience. Upon completing their studies, students will be equipped with a robust skill set that prepares them to excel in various high-demand fields, including:

• Artificial Intelligence: Students will develop intelligent systems that learn and adapt, paving the way for innovations across industries.
• Data Science: With a strong foundation in data analysis and statistics, students will be able to extract valuable insights from complex datasets to inform decision-making.
• Cybersecurity: Graduates will be prepared to protect information systems from security breaches and cyber threats, ensuring data integrity and privacy.
• Quantitative Finance: They will apply mathematical models and computational techniques to solve complex financial problems and develop effective investment strategies.
• Pharmaceutical Research: Graduates can contribute to drug discovery and development processes, utilizing their analytical skills to improve healthcare outcomes.
• Software Engineering: Equipped with programming knowledge and software development skills, students will create efficient and scalable software solutions.

This educational foundation will enable them to innovate and thrive, making significant contributions to their chosen fields.
Admission
Admission to the B.Tech/B.Tech-MS programme will be based on performance in the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE - Main) conducted by National Testing Agency(NTA) (subject to change if any ordered by Ministry of Education, Govt. of India) through the counselling under JoSAA/CSAB Click here for details...

The number of seats in each branch of the B.Tech/B.Tech-MS programme for which admission is to be made will be decided by its Academic Advisory Committee/Senate. Seats are reserved for candidates belonging to the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, Other Backward Classes, EWS Category and Physically challenged candidates as per the guidelines set by the Government of India.
