Research Groups

We have 15 Research Groups, which consist of
  1. Faculty members of IIIT Kottayam
  2. Ph.D. scholars
  3. MTech students
  4. BTech students
  5. Alumni of IIIT Kottayam
  6. Interns
  7. Industry experts
  8. Experts from R&D labs
  9. Renowned faculty members of premier institutes in India
  10. Foreign Renowned faculty members
  11. Foreign industry experts
  12. Foreign Research Scholars
  13. Foreign R&D Experts and so on.
They are developing the vibrant research and innovation culture of IIIT Kottayam. We are developing IIIT Kottayam around Research Groups. These research groups will help IIIT Kottayam to build a better Brand Name through an Active Industry interface as well as a vibrant International Interface to achieve the following goals:
  1. To enhance the Research & Project Funding for IIIT Kottayam & to establish Research Lab for the respective Group
  2. To enhance the Consultancy work for faculty members
  3. To enhance Campus Placement
