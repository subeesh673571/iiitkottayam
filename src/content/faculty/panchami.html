      OTHER RESEARCH ACTIVITIES:
      As the Faculty In-charge and Founder of CyberLabs IIIT Kottayam: Advanced Cryptology and Quantum-Safe Communication Research (CyberLabs), In-House Projects Managed and Ongoing:
      • ALPHA (Active Learning for Practical Hacking Approaches): This project envisions the creation of a Cyber Range — a dedicated, immersive environment for hands-on offensive security training. ALPHA will provide students and professionals with realistic scenarios to practice ethical hacking techniques, vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and red team exercises.
      • DRAGON (Defensive Response & Analytics for Guarding, Observation, and Neutralisation): This initiative aims to develop an integrated platform combining the functions of a Security Operations Center (SOC), Network Operations Center (NOC), and Extended Detection and Response (XDR) system. DRAGON is designed to offer next-generation capabilities in monitoring, threat detection, incident response, and real-time analytics — making it a powerful tool for cybersecurity defense and operational continuity.
